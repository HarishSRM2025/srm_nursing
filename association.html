Student Nurses Association (SNA)

The Student Nurses Association (SNA) is a nationwide organization formed by TNAI to support nursing students in their professional and personal development. It provides a platform for leadership, collaboration, and extracurricular engagement.

Established in 2018 at SRMTCON under the guidance of Dr. Suja Suresh, the SNA unit promotes student participation in academic, social, cultural, and professional activities.

Aims

To support the holistic development of nursing students and prepare them for professional roles through active engagement and leadership.

Objectives

  • Uphold dignity and honor of nursing profession
  • Promote participation in community activities
  • Encourage intercollegiate cultural and sports activities
  • Build collaborative healthcare relationships
  • Develop leadership and confidence
  • Enhance professional and social growth

SNA Office Bearers Responsibilities

Vice President

  • Preside meetings and supervise committees
  • Organize SNA programmes
  • Represent students without bias
  • Coordinate with advisor and president

Secretary

  • Assist Vice President
  • Manage meetings and records
  • Organize events
  • Report activities to advisor

Treasurer

  • Manage funds and accounts
  • Conduct fundraising
  • Maintain financial records
  • Present audited reports

Alumni Association

Aims

To maintain lifelong relationships and support institutional growth.

Rules & Regulations
