Code of Conduct for Students

General institutional rules applicable to all enrolled students

15 Rules
Identity & Attendance
1
Each student must sign an undertaking to uphold the college code of conduct at the time of admission.
2
All students are required to carry their valid identity cards to the college and clinicals at all times.
3
Students without ID cards will not be permitted to attend classes on the college premises.
4
Students must attend classes five minutes earlier each day without exception.
Behaviour & Discipline
5
All students are expected to behave courteously and fairly with everyone, inside and outside the college campus.
6
Ragging in any form is banned in the college, hostel, clinical areas, and on college transportation.
7
Follow the college-prescribed dress code at all times on campus and clinicals.
8
Be a good role model to junior students and maintain a spirit of mentorship.
Campus Rules
9
Maintain silence in class, library, and corridors at all times unless otherwise directed.
10
Take care of all college and hospital property. Damage or loss will be charged to the student.
11
Use of mobile phones is strictly prohibited during class hours, clinicals, and examinations.
12
Students must attend classes and clinicals regularly as per the schedule published by the college.
Permissions & Exits
13
No student may leave without the approval of the HOD or relevant staff member on duty.
14
Any change of address must be reported to the principal immediately for institutional records.
15
Students cannot attend meetings or excursions without prior written principal approval.

Dress Code

Uniform and appearance standards for all students

Mandatory
Boys – Uniform Standards
1
Wear formal pants and shirts only. No casual or sportswear permitted.
2
T-shirts and jeans are strictly not allowed on campus or clinicals.
3
Wear black formal shoes only — no sneakers or slippers.
4
Maintain a neat haircut and clean-shaven beard at all times.
5
Wear clean ironed uniform coat during clinical postings.
6
Shirt must be tucked in neatly and properly at all times.
Girls – Uniform Standards
1
Neatly ironed coat with clean uniform must be worn every day.
2
Small ear studs only are permitted — no dangling or large earrings.
3
Only churidhar and salwar with dupatta is the approved attire.
4
Jeans and short churidhar are strictly not allowed.
5
Hair must be neatly tied with black clips — no loose hair in clinical areas.
6
Nails must be kept short with no nail polish of any colour.
7
No jewellery permitted in clinical areas — patient safety is paramount.
8
Black formal shoes only in clinical areas. No coloured footwear.
9
No hair colouring, highlights, or bleaching is permitted.

Anti-Ragging Rules

Zero-tolerance policy — violations attract severe institutional action

Zero Tolerance

Ragging is a Criminal Offence

Any form of ragging — physical, verbal, or psychological — is strictly prohibited and will be dealt with as per AICTE and UGC anti-ragging regulations. Complaints are treated with complete confidentiality.

Administrative Action
1
Students found guilty of ragging will be punished as per AICTE and UGC anti-ragging regulations.
2
An anti-ragging committee will determine punishment after due enquiry and verification of facts.
3
All complaints of ragging will be treated with strict confidentiality and acted upon immediately.
Punishments May Include
Expulsion from institution
Suspension from hostel
Cancellation of admission
Debarring from exams
Withdrawal of scholarship
Withholding results
Suspension from representation
Fine as decided
